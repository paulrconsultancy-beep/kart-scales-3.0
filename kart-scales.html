Calibration

Do this once per scale. With the pad empty, tap Set zero. Then place a weight you know exactly (e.g. 20.0 kg of plates) on that pad, enter it and tap Calibrate. Settings are saved on this phone.

Connection setup

Use this the first time you connect. After connecting, pick the data channel whose bytes change when you press on the pad, then choose how to read the number. Your choices are remembered.
